## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I use backlighter?

Simply open the app, select a color, and tap the screen to go fullscreen. Position your phone to light your subject and take your photo with another device.

### Can I use custom colors?

Yes! You can choose from our preset colors or pick any custom color using the color picker.

### Does it work on all iPhones?

backlighter works on all iPhones running iOS 15 or later.

### How do I maximize brightness?

The app automatically suggests maximizing your screen brightness when you go fullscreen for optimal lighting.
